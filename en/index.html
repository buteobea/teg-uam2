Listening to, measuring and conserving terrestrial ecosystems

We are the Terrestrial Ecosystem Ecology and Conservation Research Group at the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid. We study how steppes, grasslands, farmland and islands work, and how to keep their biodiversity going.
